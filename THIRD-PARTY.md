# Third-party content

This repository contains no third-party source code and no copies of third-party model weights, datasets or papers.

The research cards summarize, in the author's own words, publicly available information about third-party models, datasets, tools, papers and products (for example Hugging Face model cards, GitHub READMEs, papers and vendor pages). Every card lists the source URLs it relies on. Those works are owned by their authors and remain under their own licenses and terms of use; this repository's CC BY 4.0 applies only to the author's own compilation, summaries and decisions.

Short identifiers, names, titles and URLs of third-party works are reproduced to identify them. Licenses recorded for third-party models are what a source stated at collection time (October 2026) and were not re-verified; check the linked source before relying on a license.

Raw fetched page texts and per-record fetch dossiers that were produced while researching were deliberately left out of this repository.

If you are the owner of a listed work and want an entry corrected or removed, open an issue.
